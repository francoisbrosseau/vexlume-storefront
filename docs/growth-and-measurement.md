# First ten sales / preparation

Lead with one focused customer problem: preparing a home for photography. Build useful search content around room preparation, photography-day mistakes and a 48-hour checklist. Product screenshots and an accurate sample should do more work than decorative claims.

After launch authorization, create a small Pinterest set from actual checklist pages with clear landing-page links. Invite relevant HAVEN referrals and WILDWIRE contextual mentions only under separate permission. Avoid modifying sibling sites automatically. Do not launch paid advertising before real conversion data exists.

Keep the weekly planner freely available. Add optional email subscription only with a connected provider, explicit marketing consent, unsubscribe flow and an updated privacy notice. Do not gate the existing free download or imply download consent authorizes marketing.

## Measurement schema (no data collected yet)

Daily record: date, source/medium/campaign, visitors, product_page_views, free_downloads, subscribers, checkout_starts, purchases, gross_revenue_usd, platform_fees_usd, processor_fees_usd, refunds_usd, other_costs_usd.

Derived values: purchase_conversion = purchases / visitors (null when visitors=0); checkout_conversion = purchases / checkout_starts (null when starts=0); net_proceeds = gross_revenue - platform_fees - processor_fees - refunds; operating_profit = net_proceeds - other_costs. Label profit before income tax and owner compensation unless those costs are recorded. Never fill missing data with estimates presented as actuals.

Use provider records as the purchase source of truth. A static checkout-link click means an outbound click, not a confirmed checkout or purchase. Attribute cross-device visits cautiously; account for currency and timing differences when reconciling.
