# VEXLUME / Identity refinement 02

Independent spirit. Considered design.

A gold monogram and prominent serif masthead connect VEXLUME to HAVEN's premium atmosphere. Dark walnut, golden dusk, deeper charcoal and a stronger editorial composition connect it to WILDWIRE. VEXLUME keeps its own purpose: practical, beautiful tools for home, mind and money.

## What changed

A cinematic full-width hero, shared masthead/navigation/footer across all 14 pages, real editorial photographs in the product collection, a fuller property-guide feature, an actual planner-page preview, clearer availability and a more deliberate brand-family section. No sibling website was modified. Paid downloads stay private; checkout remains disabled.

## Original image assets

All four images were generated with the built-in image tool, then optimized as WebP. They are illustrative brand photography, not claimed client projects or testimonials. Existing product covers remain editable SVGs; unreleased products remain marked in development.

- `dist/assets/hero-v2.webp`: cinematic apartment at golden dusk; olive velvet, walnut, dark stone, open journal; quiet dark left third for typography; no text/logos. Prompt direction: photorealistic-natural, premium editorial hero, architect-designed apartment, atmospheric old-city windows, rich bronze highlights, 16:9.
- `dist/assets/property-editorial.webp`: a carefully arranged living room with cream curved sofa, cognac chair, arched window, sage curtains and brass lamp. Prompt direction: photorealistic-natural, home preparation guide editorial image, Architectural Digest quality, tactile detail, natural late-afternoon light, portrait 4:5, no text/logos.
- `dist/assets/finance-editorial.webp`: ivory notebook, brass pen, laptop, espresso and sage linen on walnut. Prompt direction: photorealistic-natural, bold diagonal overhead still life, financial organization context, premium natural light and charcoal shadows, portrait 4:5, no printed words, numbers, banknotes or logos.
- `dist/assets/reflection-editorial.webp`: over-shoulder writing at a rain-streaked window at blue hour. Prompt direction: photorealistic-natural, authentic hands and journal, charcoal knit, warm brass lamp and distant city lights, face not visible, portrait 4:5, no readable writing or logos.

Each has a smaller responsive WebP variant. `dist/assets/weekly-planner-preview.webp` is rendered from the actual planner PDF, not generated artwork. The opening room image remains in use as the free-planner context.

## Validation and limitations

`docs/qa-identity-v2.json` records static checks across all pages, assets and routes. Menu/filter/search behavior was tested in the Node harness. Browser/device visual QA remains unavailable because the required control-browser skill is absent; no device-testing claim is made. Checkout and contact submissions are not operational. The existing free planner and sample links remain real downloads.
